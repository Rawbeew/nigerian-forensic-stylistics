i. Metaphors  
The poet employs metaphors in his poetry, infusing layers of meaning 
and depth into the exploration of historical reflections in Nigeria. The 
Metaphors serve as powerful linguistic devices, allowing the poet to 
convey complex ideas, emotions, convey historical narratives, and evoke 
collective memory.

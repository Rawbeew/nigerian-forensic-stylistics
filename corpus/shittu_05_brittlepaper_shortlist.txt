This year’s shortlist features: Stoning the Wind by Gbemisola Adeoti Japa: Elegy for Nigerians by Toyin Shittu Girls and the Silhouette of Form by Star Zahra With Girls and the Silhouette of Form, Zahra has not only expanded and matured her poetic voice but also made history as the first female poet published by Masobe Books. The collection has been described as an important book, particularly considering the rarity of women’s voices in contemporary Nigerian poetry publishing. The ANA KMVL Poetry Prize is one of the most important recognitions of contemporary Nigerian poetry, highlighting voices that are both innovative and deeply rooted in the nation’s literary tradition. The prize carries a cash award of ₦500,000, making it one of the most substantial poetry prizes in Nigeria. The Association of Nigerian Authors (ANA) was founded in 1981 by the legendary Nigerian novelist Chinua Achebe, who served as its founding president. Since its establishment, ANA has been the primary representative body for Nigerian creative writers both at home and abroad, playing a crucial role in nurturing, promoting, and celebrating Nigerian literature across all genres. The ANA KMVL Poetry Prize is part of a broader suite of literary awards that ANA administers annually. The organization currently manages 14 literary prize categories, covering both published and unpublished works across all genres of literature. These prizes are open to Nigerian writers of all ages, both at home and in the diaspora, with cash prizes ranging from ₦50,000 to $2,500.

Statement of Problem: 
Nigeria's historical trajectory, marked by the struggle for independence, 
internal conflicts, and economic fluctuations, presents a labyrinthine 
narrative that demands careful examination. How do linguistic devices 
employed by Toyin Shittu in his poems con tribute to a deeper 
understanding of the historical reflections encapsulated within the 
verses of "Yesterday," "Today," and "Tomorrow"? What insights do 
metaphors, irony, personification, allusion, symbolism, and imagery 
provide into the multifaceted layer s of Nigeria's past, present, and 
future? These questions propel the inquiry into the stylistic nuances that 
illuminate the historical tapestry woven the poetic expression. 
 
Explanation of the Poems: 
Toyin Shittu's three poems, "Yesterday," "Today," and "Tomorrow," 
collectively depict Nigeria's historical journey. "Yesterday" reflects on 
the struggles for liberation, exploring internal conflicts post -freedom. 
"Today" contrasts past prosperity with cont emporary challenges, 
symbolising the paradox of progress amid conflict. "Tomorrow" looks 
ahead with a mix of hope and uncertainty, using irony and allusion to 
convey the desire for a peaceful and prosperous future. Throughout, the 
poet employs metaphors, i rony, symbolism, and personification to 
create a lyrical exploration of Nigeria's past, present, and future within 
the context of historical reflections. This study endeavours to shed light 
on the intricate relationship between linguistic expression and 
historical consciousness, inviting readers to navigate the labyrinth of 
time alongside Toyin Shittu's poetic exploration. Through this 
endeavour, this study aims to contribute to the broader discourse on the 
intersection of literature, history, and the cultural identity of nations. 
 
Objectives of the Study: 
i. Examine how Linguistic Devices such as metaphors, irony, 
personification, and symbolism are strategically employed in 
Toyin Shittu's poetry.

Theoretical Framework 
The theoretical foundation of this research is grounded in Lev 
Vygotsky's (1978) cultural-historical theory, emphasising the intricate 
link between language and cognition within sociocultural contexts. 
Vygotsky's perspective views literary works as cultura l artifacts that 
reflect the complex relationship of language, thought, and societal 
dynamics. By applying Vygotsky’s framework to analyse linguistic 
devices in Toyin Shittu’s poetry, insight is gained into how these devices 
are not only shaped by but also  actively shape Nigeria's historical 
narrative. 
According to Vygotsky's social constructivism, cultural and historical 
contexts have a big impact on how people use language and think. As a 
form of cultural expression, Shittu's poetry serves as a rich collection 
that reflects the complex socio -historical substance of Nigeria. This 
theoretical framework makes it possible to investigate the ways in 
which linguistic devices such as personification, metaphors, irony, 
allusion, symbolism, and imagery help to create and disseminate a 
collective historical consc iousness. Through the use of Vygotsky's 
cultural-historical lens, the study seeks to unveil the layers of meaning 
that are embedded in linguistic devices in order to improve 
comprehension of the complex interplay of language, culture, and 
history in the Nigerian context.

Methodology 
This study employs a qualitative research design to conduct an in-depth 
exploration of the linguistic devices employed by Toyin Shittu in his 
poetry and their role in conveying historical reflections. The qualitative 
approach is particularly suitable for t he nuanced analysis required to 
unravel the layers of meaning within the poetic expressions. 
The primary data for this research comprises select poems from Toyin 
Shittu’s collection, Naija Blues. The choice of poems is purposive, guided 
by their thematic relevance to historical narratives and the consistent 
use of linguistic devices, including metaphor, irony, personification, 
allusion, symbolism, and imagery. A comprehensive textual analysis will 
be conducted to identify and catalog linguistic devices within the 
selected poems. The analysis will involve close reading, line -by-line 
scrutiny, and ca tegorization of metaphors, ironies, personifications, 
allusions, symbols, and imagery.

Examining the poetry of Toyin Shittu, especially from his collection

devices and historical allusions within the framework of Nigeria's 
fluttering past. Using a qualitative research desi gn, the analysis 
concentrated on three key poems, "Yesterday," "Today," and 
"Tomorrow," revealing their complex layers. 
The study revealed that the poet deploys metaphors to encapsulate 
Nigeria's struggles, progress, and paradoxes. These metaphors act as 
powerful snapshots, vividly conveying the nation's journey for 
liberation, economic prosperity, and the uncertainties of the future. In

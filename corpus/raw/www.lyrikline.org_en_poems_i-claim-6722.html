I claim

there is a morning in your eyes
that’s a wake of dying desire
almost ashy in the ember of my soul

its dew nourishes a tomorrow
used to be faint on shoulders of my dream
used to be shrunk in lime of world worries

there is a Christmas in your laughter
uncelebrated for a thousand years
full of splendour and rhythms of ages

its carols boom within my soul
my blood streaming in their strung rhythms
in a film of castrated thunders of time

there is a calabash of life in your huge hips
unconsumed by lusty men
preserved in the firmament of your virgin laughter

let me lay my head
on the mount on your chest

and live my morning
celebrate my Christmas
and eat of my calabash.

Aus: Naked Sun , 2006. Audioproduktion: 2010 Literaturwerkstatt Berlin

E. E. Sule is the pen name of Dr. Sule E. Egya. He teaches Creative Writing and African Literature in English in the Department of English & Literary Studies, University of Abuja, Abuja, Nigeria.

He has published a number of short stories, poems, and literary essays in journals and e-journals. He has also published three volumes of poetry and two collections of short stories. His latest poetry volume, What the Sea Told Me (2009) won the 2009 ANA/NDDC Gabriel Okara Prize for Poetry and the 2009 AWF/Anthony Agbo Prize for Poetry.

photo © private
He has just completed work on his first novel, which he began during a nine-month writing fellowship in 2006 under the mentorship of Ayi Kwei Armah. He is currently an Alexander von Humboldt postdoctoral research fellow and resident scholar at Humboldt University, Berlin.
